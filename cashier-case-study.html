01 · Context

Polish-licensed iGaming has strict rules that generic cashiers get wrong. Under Poland’s Ustawa hazardowa, licensed operators (like STS, Fortuna, or Superbet) must operate strictly in PLN, exclude crypto and anonymous vouchers, charge 0% cashier fees, enforce daily responsible-gaming limits, verify ID + PESEL before any payout, and route withdrawals exclusively to the player’s own verified Polish bank account (PL IBAN). I designed a cashier that respects every legal constraint without feeling like paperwork.

02 · The Problem

The initial prototype looked international, missed Polish regulatory rules, and broke user trust around fees and payouts.

PROBLEM 01

Unlawful / unrealistic rails & fees

Crypto, Skrill/Neteller, Paysafecard, and 2–3% player deposit fees were included—none of which match Polish-licensed operators where deposits and withdrawals are 0% fee in PLN.
PROBLEM 02

Missing KYC & PL IBAN withdrawal gate

Players could withdraw to arbitrary new card numbers without PESEL/ID verification or closed-loop Polish IBAN ownership matching.
PROBLEM 03

Disconnected bonus & RG limits

Active 35× bonus wagering wasn’t surfaced before withdrawal, and statutory daily deposit limits (Art. 15f) weren’t enforced at amount entry.
PROBLEM 04

Incomplete BLIK & SCA edge cases

BLIK lacked its 2-minute code expiry countdown and 1-Click returning-player opt-in, while cards lacked 3D Secure confirmation context.

06 · Fast / Careful

Zero friction where conversion happens. Deliberate friction where Polish law and money safety require it.

Fast ⚡

  • PLN-only without clutter or hidden Crypto tabs
  • Quick presets (50 / 100 / 250 / 500 PLN) + 1-tap repeat
  • Remember for BLIK 1-Click returning UX
  • Instant Express Elixir payouts (0% fee)
  • Back to game from any pending state

Careful 🔒

  • Mandatory ID + PESEL gate before first withdrawal
  • Strict 28-char PL IBAN validation (closed-loop AML)
  • Active bonus wagering & forfeit warning on Withdraw
  • Daily responsible-gaming deposit limit enforcement
  • BLIK 2:00 timer + “Nothing was charged” on failure

07 · States, not just screens

Every money state is designed — pending, delayed, failed, canceled, expired — each saying plainly whether anything moved.

① Top-up: X + Back to game.
② Paid: toast + new balance.
③ Withdraw: verified PL IBAN rails.
④ Out: timeline + receipt.
⑤ History: borderless ledger.
⑥ Empty: Deposit resolves it.
